Name: Apostolos Email: asakellariou@deicollege.gr Automatic speech recognition is a technology that lets computers understand human speech.It basically changes spoken words into text, this type of technology is used in many modern systems like phones, apps and more, its very common in our days. ASR has become very useful in everyday life. Many people use voice assistants like Siri on iphones to search for information ,send messages or even control devices and it makes their tasks faster and easier. It is also used in customer service systems where people can speak instead of typing so it even helps with jobs making communication easier.It can even help people with disabilities like people who cant type can use their voice to control a device as a computer or a phone. In education, ASR is used to create subtitles or make a speech into text, which helps students understand lessons better.However, ASR is not perfect. Sometimes it makes mistakes, especially with different accents or background noise. There are also privacy concerns because voice data can be recorded but this is a bit complicated because it doesn't really mean that its used against them, but still some people are concered about it. So in general, ASR is a very helpful technology that improves communication between humans and machines. It makes daily life easier but it still needs some improvements.
