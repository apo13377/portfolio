Honeypots. Benefits and disadvantages Introduction Honeypots are fake computer systems used in cybersecurity to attract hackers and study their actions Benefits Honeypots help detect cyber attacks early, they also allow researchers to understand how hackers work and improve system security. Disadvantages Honeypots do not stop attacks directly.They can also be complex and kinda hard to set up and they need regular maintenance. Conclusion Overall,honeypots are useful tools for security research but they should be used together with other protection systems for even better security.
