Honeypot Technologies Honeypots are tools used in cybersecurity. They are fake systems that look real, but they are used to detect hackers and study their behaviour. Honeypots are useful because they help security experts understand how attacks happen in real systems. When an attacker tries to access the honeypot, everything they do is recorded. This information is used to improve security systems. There are different types of honeypots. Low interaction honeypots are simple and collect basic attack data. High interaction honeypots are more advanced and give detailed information about attackers. SSH honeypots are used to study attacks on servers, while adaptive honeypots can change their behaviour depending on the attacker. Honeypots are used in both industry and research. Companies use them to improve network security and detect threats early.Researchers use them to study how hackers work and develop better protection systems. However honeypots do not stop attacks directly. They only observe and collect information. Because of this they are usually used together with other security tools References Spitzner, L.
