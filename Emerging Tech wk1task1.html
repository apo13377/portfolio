Task 2 Patient Solution

Problem

The patient is in London and the doctor is in Sunderland. The patient needs to talk with the doctor, pay for medicine and get it delivered fast.

Solution

The solution is to use online tools. The patient can talk to the doctor using a video call. The doctor sends the prescription online to the pharmacy.

The patient pays online using a safe payment system. Then the pharmacy prepares the medicine and sends it with delivery service.

The patient receives the medicine at home without going outside.

How it works

First the patient books an online appointment. Then the doctor checks the patient on video call. After that the prescription is sent to the pharmacy. The patient pays online and the medicine is delivered.
