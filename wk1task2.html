Task 2 Patient Solution

Problem

The patient is in London and the doctor is in Sunderland. The patient needs to talk to the doctor, pay for medicine, and get it delivered fast.

Solution

We use online video calls for the doctor, online payment for medicine, and a fast delivery system for the pharmacy.

Process

The patient books an online appointment. The doctor sees the patient using a video call. The doctor sends a digital prescription to the pharmacy. The patient pays online in a secure way. The pharmacy prepares the medicine and sends it using delivery. The patient receives the medicine at home.

Explanation

This system helps the patient get medical help without going to the hospital. Everything is done online so it is faster and easier. It also helps when delivery services are busy because the system can manage orders better.
